Rules for the Fridge

 

Raw meat should go on the bottom solid shelf – this stops blood from running onto the other food 
Vegetables should go in the baskets/drawers in the bottom 
Milk and bottles of juice, water etc should go in the door sections – to stop leakages onto the other food products 
Dairy products should go on the top shelf or middle shelf 
Cooked meats should be on a separate shelf but should always be covered 
Tins and dry goods should go in the cupboard 
Food should NEVER be left in the fridge or anywhere else in an opened tin (NEVER EVER EVER – highly dangerous) – any leftovers from a  tin should be put in a container and stored in the fridge 
some jars of food require you to leave them in the fridge after opening – this isn’t a message that is important to younger children
